This policy explains how the Work Timesheet app (the “App”) handles your data.

1. Operator

The App is provided by an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact us at the email below.

2. What data the App processes

  • Work records: date, arrival and departure times, lunch break, work location, jobs/orders, work description.
  • Settings: company name and address, default times, job list, cell colors, filters and display preferences.
  • Optionally: employee name (if you enter it).
  • For purchases: license / trial status and purchase token via Google Play Billing (processed by Google).

3. Where data is stored

Timesheet data and settings are stored locally on the phone. We do not send them to our servers for advertising or third-party analytics.

4. Backup and export

You create backups and exports (PDF / Excel). Files are saved according to your choice. You are responsible for their further storage.

5. Voice input

The microphone is used only if you start voice input and grant a separate consent. Speech is recognized by on-device / system services; the transcript is processed in the App.

6. Payments

The one-time full version purchase goes through Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google under their terms.

7. Internet

The App may need internet for Google Play Billing or system services. Everyday timesheet use works offline.

8. Sale and advertising

We do not sell your data or use it for personalized third-party ads.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it, uninstall the App, or restore from a backup. Uninstalling removes local App data (except files you saved outside the App).

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the App (Settings) and on this page.

By using the App you confirm that you have been informed of this policy.
